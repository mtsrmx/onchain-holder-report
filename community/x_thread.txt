RBNT holder report, Q3 2026 (1 July 2026 to 30 September 2026). We read every balance on the Redbelly chain at the start and end of the quarter to show how holders behaved. Trends in groups, never individuals. Thread.
---
1. Staking grew. The two Reddex RBNT pools held 754.8 million RBNT on 30 September, up from 639.1 million on 30 June (+18.1%). Pool balances include some reward funds; staked RBNT alone went from 635.8 million to 751.3 million.
---
2. Vesting contracts paid out. Contracts whose verified code is a vesting manager went from 1.68 billion to 1.58 billion RBNT, and sent 127.8 million to wallets during the quarter.
---
3. The 21 wallets that started with 10 million RBNT or more held 1.06 billion then and 1.09 billion at the end. 9 added more than 1%, 7 held within 1%, 5 reduced.
---
4. Holdings stay concentrated. The largest 100 wallets hold 76.7% of all wallet-held RBNT (75.8% on 30 June). 53,574 wallets held 1 RBNT or more, up from 53,417.
---
5. Redbelly's nine reserved wallets went from 5.97 billion to 5.93 billion RBNT. Most of the movement was between reserved wallets; only Ecosystem (Unlocked) sent RBNT outside the group.
---
6. Every number comes from the chain at blocks 3,025,809 and 3,209,414, and every RBNT is accounted for: 99.9996% of the 10 billion supply was found in the balances read. Not financial advice.
