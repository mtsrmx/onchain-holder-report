# RBNT holder report, Q3 2026: community summary

*1 July 2026 to 30 September 2026. Data read from Redbelly mainnet at blocks 3,025,809 and 3,209,414. Last verified 23 October 2026. Not financial advice.*

Holders often ask what the big wallets are doing. This report answers from the chain itself. We read the balance of every address at the start and end of the quarter, counted wrapped and staked RBNT with its owner, and looked at groups of wallets rather than individuals. Only addresses that are already public are named.

**Staking grew.** The two Reddex RBNT staking pools held 754.8 million RBNT at the end of the quarter, up from 639.1 million. A pool's balance includes some reward funds as well as stakes; the staked part alone went from 635.8 million to 751.3 million. 1,255 addresses had a stake at the end.

**Coins moved from vesting contracts and reserved wallets to ordinary wallets.** Vesting contracts went from 1.68 billion to 1.58 billion RBNT and sent 127.8 million to wallets. The 21 wallets that started with 10 million RBNT or more went from 1.06 billion to 1.09 billion: 9 added, 7 held steady and 5 reduced. Most smaller wallets did not change at all.

**Holdings stay concentrated.** 53,574 wallets held 1 RBNT or more on 30 September, up from 53,417. The largest 100 hold 76.7% of all wallet-held RBNT, against 75.8% at the start.

**Reserved wallets.** Redbelly's nine reserved wallets went from 5.97 billion to 5.93 billion RBNT. Ecosystem (Locked) released 500.0 million to Ecosystem 2 (Unlocked), which sent 253.1 million to Investors, and Governance (Locked) released 33.3 million to Governance (Unlocked). Ecosystem (Unlocked) was the only reserved wallet that sent RBNT outside the group, including 15.0 million to the staking pools as reward funds.

**Exchanges.** Routescan labels no exchange wallet on Redbelly, so this edition reports no exchange flows rather than guess which addresses belong to exchanges.

The chain shows that coins moved, not why. A fall in a group's holdings may be a sale, a move between a holder's own addresses or a deposit into a contract. The full report, the data behind every chart and the method are published with this summary so anyone can check the numbers and produce the next edition.
